# SOULINA English copy direction

## Source analysis

The two GetYourGuide listings provided by the owner use descriptive activity titles, second-person invitations, concrete actions and practical booking details. The useful pattern is: name the experience, explain what the visitor does, show who it suits, then state duration, location, supplies and the next step.

The landing page follows that pattern in original English copy. Headings describe content directly; no eyebrow labels or invented slogans. The brand story remains short and grounded in SOULINA's actual products and studio experiences. Product placeholders and missing event information are labeled in English.

## Page headings

1. SOULINA at Hanoi Festival of Creative Design 2026
2. Discover Vietnamese art and craft at SOULINA
3. Browse crafts and gifts at our festival booth
4. Try a creative activity at the festival
5. Lacquer art workshop in Hanoi's Old Quarter
6. Painting class with a local artist
7. Plan your visit to SOULINA
8. Have a question before you visit?

The festival activities (clay figurines and postcards) are separate from the two main studio workshops. The two studio experiences each have a dedicated section and a link to their corresponding GetYourGuide listing, with Zalo as a secondary contact route.

## Confirmed inputs and unresolved details

- Both listings show the meeting address as 27 Hang Bac, Hanoi Old Quarter.
- Lacquer: the user asks visitors to allow 1–2 hours; the listing's duration field shows 1 hour and its description mentions drying/collection time. Page copy uses the owner's 1–2 hour planning guidance without promising immediate collection.
- Painting: the URL/document title and parts of the description refer to 2 hours, while the visible H1, duration field and inclusions say 1 hour. The landing page asks the visitor to confirm duration with SOULINA rather than advertising a fixed duration.
- The listing prices and cancellation terms are not duplicated on the landing page. Visitors check the current selected listing before booking.
- Owner-provided preview links are currently used for workshop details; replace them with final public listing links before final campaign launch if appropriate.
- No reviews, ratings or participant limits are reproduced in this landing page.
- Festival dates, booth number, fees, times and five photo placeholders remain pending.

## UI and implementation

The approved poster direction is retained. Eight sections alternate expressive red, quiet cream, a flat product catalogue, a yellow festival activity section, a split lacquer photo/information layout, a reverse painting collage layout, practical visit/FAQ information and a final contact band.

Stack: buildless HTML, CSS Grid/Flex and vanilla JavaScript. No new frontend library. Native disclosure controls for product details/FAQ, visible-by-default entrance motion using IntersectionObserver, reduced-motion support and responsive menu. Full page defaults to English; the two archived design studies retain their original copy.
